# Code of conduct

We want Fireworks Open ELI5 to be a focused, respectful, and technically honest
place to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Make room for different levels of experience and different languages.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized
content, doxxing, and retaliation are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the community. For immediate
platform abuse, use GitHub's private report/blocking tools. For a
project-specific private concern, contact the repository owners through the
repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel and identify the report as
a conduct matter.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, review threads,
and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
